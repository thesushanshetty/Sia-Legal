02 / ABOUT

THE LAWYER BEHIND SIA

A first-generation lawyer.
A deliberately independent practice.

Sushan Gangadhar Shetty is an Advocate practising before the Bombay High Court, with a professional focus on Criminal & Family Law, Cyber Cases, and Arbitration & Mediation.

His journey into law was not inherited. It was built through education, professional experience and a conscious decision to enter the legal profession as a first-generation lawyer.

SIA Legal was founded with a simple premise: legal advice should begin with understanding the matter properly, identifying the real issue and choosing a strategy that is proportionate to the client's circumstances.
